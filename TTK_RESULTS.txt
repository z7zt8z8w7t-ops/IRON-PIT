IRON PIT v37 — VIRTUAL WEAPON TEST RESULTS

Actual game simulation at 60 Hz, 700-health stationary tracked target, standard modifiers, shields and repairs off. Centre-to-centre distances of 300 / 500 / 800 units. No obstacles, correct muzzle alignment, missiles supplied a target lock. Heat, cooling, reloads, charge, projectile travel, armour and burn are included. Harpoon physically pulls the target. Mines are measured behind the shooter. Time starts at first firing command, including Smart Gun startup and plasma/mortar charge/flight.
Timing resolution approximately 0.017 seconds. This is ideal aiming, not a prediction for a moving opponent, manual two-thumb operation or terrain/forcefield interference.
Three trials for flak, swarm, mortar, mines and barrage; one for deterministic weapons. Results below are means. No kill means no destruction within 180 seconds. Smoke is non-damaging. Mines only reach close targets.

OBSERVATIONS
Railgun is the fastest individual weapon in this stationary-target test (~7.6s), followed by pulse lasers (~8.9s).
The Smart Gun (~15.8s) trades raw damage and weight for aim assistance. Harpoon (~45.3s) is a utility weapon, not a damage leader.
EMP (~136.7s) and smoke are specialist tools: direct damage TTK alone does not value shield disruption or visual cover.
Flak loses damage at longer distances. Flame and arc have limited reach. Mines need targets to cross their deployed area.
Pulse + railgun + hunter has an extremely fast ideal burst (~2.6s at medium distance). This combination deserves playtesting before further damage changes.
These figures describe the current v37 damage values. No automatic rebalance was applied after testing.
