IRON PIT v39 — VIRTUAL WEAPON TEST RESULTS

Actual game simulation at 60 Hz against a stationary 700-health tracked target, standard modifiers, no shields, repairs or obstacles. TTK includes reloads, cooling, armour, charge, projectile travel and burn. Accurate muzzle alignment; guided missile target lock. Harpoon pulls the target. Mines target behind shooter. Distances are centre-to-centre.
One trial per deterministic weapon; three for flak/swarm/mortar/mines/barrage. TTK is the mean. NO KILL means no kill within 180 seconds. These are ideal firing tests, not moving-opponent or two-thumb combat predictions.
Weights, firing rates, reload times and heat/cooling settings remain unchanged. Damage was calibrated to make combat weapons progressively stronger in unlock order. Discrete volleys and heat pauses prevent exact target times. Harpoon and EMP are utility; Smoke does no damage.
