IRON PIT v91 — NEW STANDS, TRAINING AND ROOKIE FIXTURES

Install this patch OVER v90 in the existing game folder, replacing matching files. Deploy the updated folder as usual and reload the game. The offline cache identifies itself as v91. This package does not include unchanged v90 ground textures and audio.

The approved oval spectator stands are now shared by all ten competition arena types. The playable floor retains the v90 size and uniform scale: 1800 x 2400 units, twelve equal 600 x 600 cells, no visible grid. Surrounding stadium artwork fills the arena canvas in both dimensions, with outer seating cropped/extended to fit the screen. Overhanging balcony art remains a separate foreground layer, and towers keep their separate collision coordinates and small stone surrounds.

Dave's Proving Ground is an additional private workshop arena used for training and the Rookie Qualifier. Its overhead service gantry replaces spectator seating. Training introduces movement, turret aiming, weapons, shield, repair, forcefields, four-tower hazard warnings, escape, a practice fight, then helicopter extraction. Checkpoints are in clear floor cells. Hazards are disabled outside their lesson. Tutorial text and control highlighting remain stage-specific.

The approved new five-row Rookie template contains the cinematic arena illustrations, approved portraits and actual game-rendered mechs. Initially only the Qualifier is revealed. Every win reveals the next round. Future rows show AWAITING RESULTS and do not load/render opponent portraits, arena images, mech loadouts or reward labels. Completed rows remain available for replay. Weapon awards are unchanged. The longer Challenger league uses the same reveal rules and image-window style; its existing opponents and match modes are preserved. Ashfall and Dust Crucible are assigned to Challenger Round 3 and Semi-final so all new competition arenas remain available through progression.

Dust Crucible sandstorms, Frostbite snowstorms and Mist now render over the entire stadium canvas, including towers, balconies and stands. Mist has varied translucent layers and a light overall veil. Frostbite has intermittent stronger snowstorms with AI visibility reduction. Blackout dims the stands, tower caps, perimeter shield, internal walls and signal/floodlight artwork; mech headlights reveal the floor.

Deployment and extraction helicopters render above stadium foreground and all weather/effects. Lifted-mech lighting follows the carried mech. The Foundry motor recording retains the v90 twelve-second crossfade and helicopter-exit stop point.
